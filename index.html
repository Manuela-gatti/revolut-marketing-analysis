The 5 C's

A framework to analyse the company and its environment before making marketing decisions.

01

Company

The firm's own resources, skills, culture and goals, which show what it can realistically do.

02

Customer

Who buys, what they need and how they decide, which shows where the value lies.

03

Competitor

The direct, indirect and replacement rivals that compete for the same customer need.

04

Collaborators

The partners, suppliers and distributors that help the company create and deliver value.

05

Climate

The macro-environmental forces (PESTEL) that the company cannot control.

03 · Framework

Ansoff Matrix

Market penetration

Existing products, existing markets. Grow share, increase usage, acquire competitors' customers.

Product development

New products, existing markets. Innovate to meet current customers' evolving needs.

Market development

Existing products, new markets. Expand geographically or reach new customer segments.

Diversification

New products, new markets. Highest risk, highest reward growth path.

A diagram that maps how customers perceive different products or brands. It shows the level of fitness between our value proposition and the market.

Positioning statement

For internationally mobile people and businesses who manage money across borders, Revolut is the global all-in-one financial app that lets them spend, send, save, invest and borrow in many currencies from one account, with low exchange fees. Unlike local neobanks or single-product apps, it combines a licensed bank with a lifestyle ecosystem, so customers never need another app for their money.
